## ⚔️ THE ADVENTURE

**Text Adventure Remastered v2.0** is a complete rebuild of my original
[Text Adventure](https://github.com/Rarxan/text-adventure) project.

What started as a classic Java web application has been rebuilt from the ground up as a modern, cinematic **Spring Boot web experience** — with a new game engine, completely redesigned interface, responsive desktop and mobile layouts, immersive audio, animated environments, and branching player choices.

The player enters a hostile medieval world where every decision matters. Explore the castle, choose your path, survive deadly encounters, and find your way through the adventure.

There are no accounts, no database, and no permanent progress to manage.

**Just enter your name, step inside the castle — and try not to die.**

<br>

> **Every choice has consequences. Some of them are fatal.**
